Our direction

Why Sugilanon exists.

Mission

To inspire people to share their stories of life and faith through meaningful conversations and journaling.

Vision

To see faith and relationships flourish in small groups, friendships, and families as they share their stories through Sugilanon products.

Why we create

Because every story matters.

Stories can strengthen faith, deepen relationships, and bring us closer—to one another and to God. That's why we create meaningful ways to connect with one another, reflect on, and remember the stories we carry.

Our anchor

It started with a verse.

John 21:25

Jesus did many other things as well. If every one of them were written down, I suppose that even the whole world would not have room for the books that would be written.

If the works of Jesus could fill endless books, how many stories could be written about God's goodness in our own lives?

What we believe
We ourselves are letters.

Living letters—not written with ink, but written by Christ and inscribed by the Spirit upon our hearts.

Inspired by 2 Corinthians 3:2–3
